# Welcome to Nimbus Labs

Welcome aboard! We're really glad you're here. This handbook is your map for the first few months. Read it in any order, and ask your Onboarding Buddy (the AI assistant) or a real human whenever something is unclear. Nobody expects you to know everything on day one.

## About us

Nimbus Labs is a startup of about 120 people. We build **Nimbus Flow**, a workflow-automation SaaS product for small businesses.

With Nimbus Flow, a bakery owner can say "when a new order arrives in my online shop, add it to my spreadsheet, send the customer a thank-you email, and ping the kitchen on WhatsApp". They do it without writing any code. Each of those automations is called a *flow*.

Our customers are mostly businesses with 5–50 employees. They choose us because we are simple, affordable and reliable.

## Mission and values

Our mission: **give every small business the automation superpowers that big companies have.**

Our four values guide how we work every day:

- **Customers first.** We start from the customer's problem, not from the technology.
- **Ship small.** Small, frequent changes are safer and teach us faster than big-bang releases.
- **Own it.** If you see a problem, you own it until it's fixed or handed to the right person.
- **Be kind.** Assume good intent, give honest feedback gently, and help each other learn.

## Teams

Engineering is split into three product teams. Each team has its own Slack channel, standup and sprint board.

- **Platform:** owns the core API (`flow-api`), the workflow runner, the database and the cloud infrastructure. Slack: `#team-platform`.
- **Growth:** owns signup, billing, onboarding emails and the main web app (`flow-web`). Slack: `#team-growth`.
- **Integrations:** owns the connectors to third-party apps such as Google Sheets, Shopify and WhatsApp. Slack: `#team-integrations`.

You will mostly work inside your own team, but everyone is welcome to ask questions in any team channel.

## Who's who

These are the people you'll meet first:

- **Priya Sharma, Engineering Manager.** Your manager. You'll have a weekly 1:1 with her on Mondays to talk about goals, feedback and anything on your mind.
- **Arjun Mehta, Senior Software Engineer (Platform).** **Your onboarding buddy.** Arjun is your first stop for "how do I…?" questions about the codebase, tools and team habits. He'll pair with you on your first pull request.
- **Neha Kapoor, HR Business Partner.** Your contact for leave, payroll, benefits and any HR policy question.
- **IT Helpdesk.** Laptop, accounts and access problems. Reach them in `#it-help` on Slack.

A good rule: if you've been stuck for 30 minutes, ask Arjun. That's what he's there for.

## Your first day

Here's what your first day looks like:

1. **10:00 – Welcome session** with HR. You'll get an introduction to the company, policies and paperwork.
2. **11:00 – Laptop pickup from IT.** IT gives you your MacBook and helps you sign in to Okta, Google Workspace and Slack.
3. **13:00 – Lunch with your team.** It's on us. Get to know the people you'll be working with.
4. **15:00 – Buddy intro** with Arjun. He'll walk you through the onboarding checklist and help you start setting up your development environment.

Don't worry about writing code on day one. Your goal is to get your accounts working and meet people.
