# HR Policies

This page summarizes the HR policies you'll need most often. The full policy documents are in **PeopleHub**, our HR portal. For anything not covered here, contact **Neha Kapoor, HR Business Partner** (hr@nimbuslabs.example).

## Working hours

- **Core hours are 11:00–16:00 IST.** Be available for meetings and messages during this window. Outside it, you choose your own start and end times, as long as you work a full day.
- **Hybrid work:** we're **in the office Tuesday to Thursday**. Monday and Friday are work-from-home days.
- Put your working hours in your Google Calendar so teammates know when to reach you.
- If you can't make it to the office on an office day, tell your manager and team in your team channel.

## Leave

- **Paid leave:** you get **24 days of paid leave per year**. It's prorated in your first year based on your joining date.
- **Sick leave:** **12 days per year**. If you're unwell, message your manager before standup if you can, and apply in PeopleHub when you're back.
- **Public holidays:** we follow the yearly holiday list published in PeopleHub every December.
- **How to apply:** request leave in **PeopleHub**. Planned leave should be requested **at least 1 week in advance**. Your manager approves it there.
- Add your leave to your Google Calendar and set a Slack status so people know you're away.
- Unused paid leave: up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.

## Payroll

- **Salary is paid on the last working day of each month.**
- **During week 1,** log in to **PeopleHub** and add your **bank account details**, PAN and address. Without them, payroll can't pay you on time.
- Your payslip is available in PeopleHub on payday.
- Questions about your salary, tax deductions or payslip go to HR (hr@nimbuslabs.example).

## Probation

- The **probation period is 6 months**.
- You'll have structured **check-ins with your manager at 30, 60 and 90 days** (see *Your First 90 Days*). They are friendly conversations about how things are going, not exams.
- At the end of probation, your manager and HR confirm your employment. If anything is off track, you'll hear about it early in your check-ins, never as a surprise.

## Expenses

- Work-related expenses (approved travel, a conference ticket, team lunches you organized) are reimbursed.
- **Submit expenses within 30 days** of the purchase, with receipts, through the expenses section of PeopleHub.
- Questions about expenses go to the Finance team at finance@nimbuslabs.example.
- Laptop, monitor and other equipment come from IT. You don't need to buy them yourself.

## Benefits

- **Health insurance** covers you, your spouse or partner, and up to two children or two parents. Your insurance card is emailed to you in your first week.
- **Learning budget:** a yearly allowance for books, courses and conferences. Discuss how to use it with your manager.
- **Wellness:** an annual health check-up and access to a confidential counselling service.

## Code of conduct

We want Nimbus Labs to be a place where everyone feels safe and respected.

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
- **If you experience or witness harassment or inappropriate behavior,** report it to **HR (Neha Kapoor)** directly or by email at hr@nimbuslabs.example. Reports are handled confidentially, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation of this policy.
- If you're unsure whether something is OK, ask HR. There is no such thing as a silly question here.
